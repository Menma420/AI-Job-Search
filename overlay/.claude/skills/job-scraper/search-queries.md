# Search Queries — India New-Grad Software Engineering

## Search objective
Find live roles that a 2026 B.Tech IT graduate with substantial backend internship experience can credibly apply to now. Backend is the default priority; do not omit strong general SWE/platform/full-stack opportunities.

## Candidate search themes
Core stack: Java, Spring Boot, REST APIs, microservices, event-driven systems, AWS, SQS, PostgreSQL, Elasticsearch, Docker, Jenkins/CI-CD, Temporal, Go, C++, Python, JavaScript/TypeScript.

Strong evidence: production SDE internship at ACKO, distributed workflow/reliability work, DSA strength (1000+ problems), CodeChef 3-star, Flipkart Grid finalist.

## Freshness
Default to jobs posted in the last 14 days. Prefer 0-7 days. Include older roles only when the official posting is confirmed live and the role is unusually strong.

## Geography
Priority: Bengaluru/Bangalore, Hyderabad, Pune, Mumbai, Gurugram/Gurgaon, Noida/Delhi NCR, Chennai, Kolkata, Ahmedabad, Remote India, and other Indian locations for exceptional fit.

International roles belong in the primary list only when the posting explicitly permits India-based remote work or sponsorship/India eligibility.

## Primary eligibility terms
Search aggressively for fresher/freshers, new grad/new graduate, graduate software engineer, 2026 graduate, entry level/early career, 0-1 years, 0-2 years, Software Engineer I/1, SDE I/1, Associate Software Engineer, and software-engineering-focused graduate trainee roles.

## Hard screening rules
Primary:
- freshers/new-grad/graduate/2026 explicitly, OR
- 0-1 / 0-2 years, OR
- Bachelor's degree with no minimum experience, OR
- clearly entry-level with no conflicting requirement.

Stretch:
- 1-3 years
- 1-2 years where internship-equivalent experience may plausibly count
- 2 years preferred rather than required
- Level I title with ambiguous requirement language

Exclude from primary:
- 2+ years REQUIRED
- 3+ years
- SDE II / Software Engineer II unless explicitly entry-level
- senior/staff/lead/manager
- mandatory work authorization not satisfied
- closed/expired postings

Required years override an entry-level title.

## Result quality
Capture title, company, location, official/apply URL, posting freshness, experience requirement, primary/stretch verdict, backend relevance, top overlap and one honest gap.

Do not pad a batch to 10 with weak or ineligible jobs.
