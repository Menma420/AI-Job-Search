# /referrals — Find Referral Targets for Ranked Jobs

Use .claude/skills/referral-finder/SKILL.md as the canonical workflow.

Input forms:
- /referrals → use up to the top 5 currently ranked strong/good-fit jobs from this run.
- /referrals <company> → find targets for that company among ranked/tracked jobs.
- /referrals <job URL> → research that specific job/company.
- /referrals --top 10 → process up to 10 jobs, but still limit contacts per company.

For ranked jobs, reuse stored title/company/location/URL and strengths/gaps rather than re-fetching only to recover metadata. Verify every person-specific claim with public evidence.

Do not send LinkedIn messages. Produce exact profiles and a send-ready outreach queue.
