## Fresher / New-Grad Experience Gate — run before scoring

This candidate is graduating in 2026 and has meaningful production SDE internship experience. The primary search must remain genuinely fresher/new-grad friendly.

Classify the posting before normal fit scoring:

| Posting experience language | Verdict |
|---|---|
| Freshers / new grads / graduate program / 2026 grads explicitly welcome | PRIMARY PASS |
| 0-1 years or 0-2 years | PRIMARY PASS |
| Bachelor's degree with no minimum experience stated | PRIMARY PASS, unless contradictory language appears elsewhere |
| "1+ year" / 1-2 years | STRETCH — internship may help, but do not call it clearly eligible |
| 1-3 years | STRETCH |
| 2 years preferred / nice-to-have | STRETCH, potentially strong if stack overlap is exceptional |
| 2+ years REQUIRED, 2-4 years, 3+ years | FAIL for primary fresher list |
| Senior / Staff / Lead / Manager / SDE II with experience bar | FAIL |

Rules:
1. Required years beat the title. "Software Engineer I" with "2-4 years required" is Stretch/Fail, not a fresher role.
2. Internship work counts as relevant experience evidence in scoring, but never silently becomes fabricated full-time YOE.
3. A role that fails this gate may be shown only in a clearly labeled Stretch/Avoid section when unusually relevant.
4. Quote or faithfully summarize the experience wording in output so eligibility is auditable.
5. This gate is separate from Technical Skills Match. A 95/100 stack match does not erase a hard experience requirement.
